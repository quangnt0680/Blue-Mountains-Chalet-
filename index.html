Good to know

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Check-in is self-serve with a keypad, and we send the code before you arrive.

Is there a minimum stay?

Yes, the minimum stay is 2 nights.

How does payment work?

You pay securely online when you book. Your confirmation email shows the full amount and the cancellation policy.

Is there a damage deposit?

Yes, a refundable damage deposit of $1,000. The day before you arrive, we'll send you a secure Stripe link that places a $1,000 hold on your card. Your card isn't charged. We release the hold after checkout once we've checked the home, and your bank may take a few days to show it as released.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund. Cancel 14 to 29 days before check-in for a 50% refund. Cancellations less than 14 days before check-in aren't refunded. If you book at least 14 days before check-in, you can also cancel within 24 hours of your booking being accepted for a full refund. The policy is also shown at checkout, before you pay.

How many people can stay?

Up to 16 guests, including children. The Town of The Blue Mountains enforces this limit, with fines of $500 to $1,000.

Can we bring a pet?

Yes, up to 2 pets. There's a $100 pet cleaning fee per booking, and pets need to be added to your booking.

What are the house rules?

Quiet hours start at 10 pm, and no shoes inside, please. The hot tub is open 7 am to 11 pm under the Town bylaw. Please leave it running and put the cover back on right after each use. At the fire pit, burn only dry, seasoned firewood (no trash, plastic or treated wood), never leave the fire unattended, put it out fully with water before bed or leaving, and skip it on windy days.

Do we need winter tires?

Yes, from roughly December to April. The chalet is at the top of the mountain and the roads up can be icy.

Can I work remotely from the chalet?

There's free Wi-Fi, but cell signal is weaker at the top of the mountain, so plan calls around the Wi-Fi. It's great for unplugging.

Is the home suitable for young children?

Families are welcome, but the home isn't child-proofed. There's a deck, a hot tub, a fire pit and stairs, so please supervise children at all times.

Are there cameras or monitors?

Exterior security cameras cover the front, back and driveway. A noise monitor in the common areas measures volume only and doesn't record conversations.

Anything else we should know?

It's an older home near farmland and woods. We do regular pest control, but ladybugs or flies can get in, so please keep doors and screens closed. The BBQ works year-round, but from December to mid-April the cold can affect it, so please clean it after each use. As an older home, it can also take a little longer to warm up than a newer house.
