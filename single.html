[Full refund up to 30 days before arrival; 50% up to 14 days before.]

How many people can stay?

Up to 16 guests, including children. The Town of The Blue Mountains enforces this limit, with fines of $500 to $1,000.

Can we bring a pet?

Yes. There's a $100 cleaning fee per pet, and pets need to be added to your booking.

What are the house rules?

Quiet hours start at 10 pm, and no shoes inside, please. The hot tub is open 7 am to 11 pm under the Town bylaw. At the fire pit, burn only dry, seasoned firewood, put it out fully with water, and skip it on windy days.

Do we need winter tires?

Yes, from about December to April. The chalet is at the top of the mountain and the roads up can be icy.

Can I work remotely from the chalet?

There's free Wi-Fi, but cell signal is weaker at the top of the mountain, so plan calls around the Wi-Fi.

Is the home suitable for young children?

Families are welcome, but the home isn't child-proofed. There's a deck, a hot tub, a fire pit and stairs, so young children need an adult with them.

Are there cameras or monitors?

Exterior security cameras cover the front, back and driveway. A noise monitor in the common areas measures volume only and doesn't record conversations.

Anything else we should know?

It's an older home near farmland and woods. We do regular pest control, but ladybugs or flies can get in, so please keep doors and screens closed. In winter, the BBQ can be slow in the cold and should be cleaned after each use.
